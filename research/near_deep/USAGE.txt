Files
  protocol.json: original design frozen before deep data retrieval/model evaluation.
  stop_protocol.json: user stop clarification recorded before model results.
  data.py: causal features, hindsight labels, target-touch/path outcomes and splits.
  evaluate.py: four fixed model variants, three walk-forward folds, two baselines.
  stops.py:4/12/24h high/low plus causally confirmed15min pivots; pessimistic ties/gaps.
  antecedents.py: exploratory matched comparisons,96 hypotheses, week blocks and Holm.
  robustness.py: all structural stops, candidate coverage, postselected peer transfer.
  null_audit.py:1999 attempts for EVERY model, corrected comparison family, no refitting.
  current_rules.mjs: actual production core, explicitly approximate historical tickers.
  output/locks/: model+threshold serialized BEFORE corresponding test predictions.
  output/scores/: every eligible test candidate, not only the successful alerts.
  output/*json: all metrics, source/data hashes, version manifest and limitations.

Interpretation
  Target touch, pre-onset precision and target-before-stop are different quantities.
  Pre-onset label:15..60min before retrospective zigzag extremum, confirmation<=72h.
  The quiet-entry filter loses many events; this does NOT prove no precursor exists.
  24/72h are evaluation windows. Return calculations assume closing at horizon on
  timeout; this does not reproduce the user's actual discretionary holding period.
  Costs10/20bp and entry next-open are assumptions, not executed fills. Funding uses
  fixed entry-notional approximation and exit-bar settlement attribution. Actual
  funding order within a bar may differ in either direction. No leverage/liquidation.
  Per-alert returns can overlap; these are NOT capital/portfolio returns.
  Structural stops use trade price, not mark-price-triggered exchange execution.
  Stop variants are sensitivities, not the user's exact discretionary chart choices.
  Weekly CIs are individual, not adjusted for selecting a best trading combination.
  Random timing uses greedy same-week budgets, not a uniform/causal randomization test.
  Peer selection used recent history: its transfer results are NOT unbiased validation.
  Historical OI publication timestamp+15min is a lag assumption, not captured latency.
  Study does not include historical NEAR orderbook, taker side or liquidation flow.
